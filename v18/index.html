The difference is what it will not write

Most tools will write anything you point them at. These five rules are the reason ours will not.

Without Suara

  • ✕Posts that mention things you never said
  • ✕A tool that guesses when it runs out of facts
  • ✕“Best in town” written by someone who has never been
  • ✕Last year's price still up on a post from March
  • ✕Finding out from a customer that you claimed something you cannot

With Suara

  • ✓Every claim traces back to something you told us
  • ✓It asks you one question instead of inventing an answer
  • ✓Superlatives only survive if you gave us the proof
  • ✓No price goes out unless you marked it publishable
  • ✓Halal, allergen and licensing language is refused outright
