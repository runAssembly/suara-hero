Privacy policy.

What Suara collects about you and the business you run, why we hold it, and what you can make us do with it.

This is a scaffold, not a policy. Every section below is a placeholder describing what belongs in it. Nothing here is legal advice and none of it is in force. It needs to be written against the entity Suara actually operates through, and reviewed by a lawyer in every market it sells in, before it goes live.

1. Who we are

To be written — the legal entity that controls your data, its registered address, and how to reach the person responsible for privacy questions.

2. What we collect

To be written — account details; the business information you give us or we read from your public listing and website; the corrections and brand memory you build up; the social accounts you connect and the tokens that let us post; payment details held by the payment processor; and ordinary usage and device logs.

3. Why we hold it

To be written — each purpose tied to the specific data it needs — writing and designing your posts, publishing them to the accounts you connected, billing you, supporting you, and keeping the service secure.

4. Our legal basis

To be written — the lawful basis per purpose. This has to work for every market Suara sells in, so it needs to name the regimes that apply — consent, contract and legitimate interests under GDPR, and the equivalent grounds under Malaysia's PDPA.

5. Who we share it with

To be written — the sub-processors. This list must be real and complete: the model providers that generate text and images, the social platforms you connect, hosting, analytics, the payment processor, and support tooling.

6. How your information is used by AI models

To be written — the question every business owner will actually ask — whether their information trains anybody's model, which providers see it, and what the retention terms with those providers are. Answer it plainly.

7. How long we keep it

To be written — a retention period per category, and what happens to your brand memory and your posts after you cancel.

8. Your rights

To be written — access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and withdrawing consent — plus how to exercise each, and which regulator to complain to.

9. Sending data between countries

To be written — where the data physically sits and the mechanism relied on when it leaves that country.

10. Cookies

To be written — what is set, what each one does, and how to refuse the non-essential ones.

11. How we protect it

To be written — the actual controls — encryption, access control, and what we do if there is a breach and when you would hear about it.

12. Children

To be written — the minimum age for an account and that the service is not aimed at children.

13. Changes to this policy

To be written — how you are told when it changes, and that material changes are notified rather than quietly published.
