This is a scaffold, not a contract. Every section below is a placeholder describing what belongs in it. Nothing here is legal advice and none of it is in force. Several clauses — liability, governing law, the content licence — depend on which entity Suara operates through and must be drafted by a lawyer.

1. Who this agreement is between

To be written — the legal entity providing the service and the business accepting these terms, and that whoever accepts them has authority to bind that business.

2. What Suara provides

To be written — the service described honestly — it writes, designs and schedules posts from information you provide, subject to your approval. Say what is included at each plan and what is not.

3. Your account

To be written — eligibility, keeping credentials secure, and responsibility for anyone you let use the account.

4. Your content and the licence you give us

To be written — you keep ownership of your photographs, text and brand. We need a limited licence to process them and publish to the accounts you connect — scoped to that and no wider, and ending when you leave.

5. Approval and publishing

To be written — the important one. Nothing publishes without your approval; once you approve, you are the publisher of that post and responsible for its content. Set out what happens when a platform rejects or removes a post.

6. Accuracy and claims

To be written — Suara refuses claims it cannot source, but it works from what you tell it — so the facts you supply are yours to stand behind, including certifications, allergen and licensing statements.

7. Third-party platforms

To be written — Instagram, Facebook and any other connected network have their own terms, which you are also bound by. We do not control their reach, their policy changes or their decisions about your account.

8. Acceptable use

To be written — what the service may not be used for, and that we can suspend an account that does it.

9. Fees, billing and cancellation

To be written — price, currency, billing cycle, what the free tier includes, how and when you can cancel, and what happens to the rest of a paid period.

10. What we do not promise

To be written — explicitly: no promise about reach, followers, engagement or sales. This protects you as much as us, and it should be plain rather than buried.

11. Our intellectual property

To be written — the software, the brand and the gate remain ours; nothing in the agreement transfers them.

12. Limitation of liability

To be written — the cap and the exclusions. Needs a lawyer — the enforceable wording differs by market and a copied clause may not survive.

13. Suspension and termination

To be written — how either side ends it, notice, and what you can take with you.

14. Changes to these terms

To be written — how changes are notified and when they take effect.

15. Governing law and disputes

To be written — the governing law and where disputes are heard. Follows directly from where the entity sits, so it cannot be written before that is settled.
