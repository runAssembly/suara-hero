Suara · What it refuses
The part nobody else does

It would rather write nothing than write something you didn’t say.

Most tools will write whatever you ask them to, and whatever reads well. Suara only writes what you told it — and everything it writes traces back to the answer it came from. These are the rules that make it refuse.

Five rules it will not break

They are not tone settings. They are the reason a post can go out with your name on it.

01

Every claim traces back to something you told us

If a sentence cannot be walked back to an answer you gave, a review you pasted or your own listing, it does not get written.

02

It asks you one question instead of inventing an answer

When it runs out of facts it stops and asks. It does not fill the gap with something plausible, and it does not quietly write around it.

03

Superlatives only survive if you gave us the proof

Best, finest, number one, award-winning. Nobody can check those from the outside, so neither can we — unless you hand us the thing that proves it.

04

No price goes out unless you marked it publishable

Prices move and old ones stay up. Suara will not put a number in a caption that you have not explicitly cleared for publishing.

05

Halal, allergen and licensing language is refused outright

These are the claims that carry a legal consequence rather than an embarrassment. They are not softened or implied — they are blocked, and you are told why.

What that looks like on a real caption

Two it refused, and one it allowed.

Blocked
“Our halal-certified kitchen…”

You never said you are certified. It will not say it either — not softened, not implied. Blocked.

Blocked
“Best kopitiam in KL”

Nobody can prove that, so it does not go out with your name on it.

Allowed
“The uncle remembers how you take it.”

Because you pasted that review. Quoted exactly, never tidied up.

And it notices things
a tool would not.

Merdeka falls on a Monday this year. If you close Mondays, it will not write “come celebrate” — it tells you the clash instead.

That noticing is the job. Anyone can write a caption.

When it does not know the rule

Every country writes its own rulebook for what a business may claim about food, health and licensing. Where Suara has not done that reading, it does not guess at it — it falls back to rule two and asks you.

The difference is what it refuses to write.
